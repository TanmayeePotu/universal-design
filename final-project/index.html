OUR PURPOSE

Accessibility isn't an extra feature.

The internet has become an essential part of our everyday lives. We use it to learn, work, communicate, and connect with others. But not everyone experiences the web in the same way.

Universal design encourages us to create websites that work for people with different abilities, needs, and experiences. Accessibility should be considered from the beginning of the design process, not added as an afterthought.

This website explores why accessibility matters, what universal design means, and how developers can create a better web for everyone.
